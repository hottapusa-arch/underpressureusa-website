Meet the Backhoe

Piper spotted a yellow machine working beside a water department truck in Riverton.

It had a big bucket on the front and a digging arm on the back.

“It's like two machines in one!”

Bolt explained that she was looking at a backhoe loader.

Two Tools, One Machine

A backhoe loader combines two useful pieces of equipment into one machine.

The front loader scoops, lifts and moves materials like dirt, sand and gravel.

The backhoe on the rear digs holes and trenches, helping workers reach underground pipes.

Staying Steady

Before digging with the backhoe, the operator lowers special legs called stabilizers.

These legs help keep the machine steady while the digging arm moves and lifts heavy materials.

Getting Around Town

Unlike many excavators that travel on tracks, backhoe loaders usually have four rubber tires.

This makes them useful for utility crews that need to travel between different job sites around Riverton.

A Utility Worker's Best Friend

Backhoe loaders can help workers uncover broken water pipes, dig trenches for new water services and move materials around a job site.

After a pipe is repaired, the front loader can help move soil back into the excavation.

It's a very useful machine!

Did You Know?

Many backhoe loaders have a seat that swivels around so the operator can face either the front loader or the rear digging arm.

Your Next Discovery

The next time you see a backhoe loader, try to spot its front bucket, digging arm, tires and stabilizers.

Remember to always watch working equipment from a safe distance.

Keep exploring! There's an amazing world beneath your feet.
