Why Should You Call Before You Dig?

Piper noticed colorful flags and painted lines across a yard in Riverton.

“Is someone decorating the grass?”

Drip laughed and explained that the markings help protect the hidden world beneath our feet.

You Can't See Everything Underground

Beneath our yards and streets are pipes and cables that carry water, natural gas, electricity and other important services.

Accidentally damaging one of these utilities can interrupt services and create dangerous situations.

Remember 811

Before starting a digging project, adults should contact 811.

This free service helps notify utility companies so they can mark the approximate locations of their underground utilities.

Even projects like planting a tree or installing a fence require careful planning.

A Rainbow of Colors

Utility workers use different colors to identify underground services.

• Blue — Drinking water.

• Green — Sewer and drain lines.

• Yellow — Natural gas and other gaseous materials.

• Red — Electricity.

• Orange — Communication lines.

• Purple — Reclaimed water.

• White — Proposed excavation area.

• Pink — Temporary survey markings.

Each color helps tell workers what's happening beneath the ground.

Finding Hidden Utilities

Utility locators use special equipment to help find underground pipes and cables.

Bolt loves learning about the technology that helps workers locate things they can't see!

Did You Know?

Some underground utilities are buried much closer to the surface than you might expect. That's why even small digging projects need to be planned safely.
