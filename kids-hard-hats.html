Why Do Workers Wear Hard Hats?

Mia noticed that everyone working at a construction site in Riverton was wearing a hard hat.

“Why do they need those funny-looking hats?”

Piper explained that hard hats are an important part of keeping workers safe.

Protecting Your Head

Construction sites can be busy places filled with heavy equipment, tools and building materials.

A hard hat helps protect a worker's head from falling objects and accidental bumps.

Unlike an ordinary baseball cap, a hard hat is specially designed to provide protection.

What's Inside a Hard Hat?

Many hard hats have a system of straps inside called a suspension.

These straps help create space between the worker's head and the hard outer shell.

This design helps absorb and spread out the force of an impact.

Bolt thinks that's some pretty clever engineering!

More Than Just Hard Hats

Workers also wear other protective equipment, depending on the job they're doing.

• Safety glasses help protect their eyes.

• Gloves help protect their hands.

• Safety boots help protect their feet.

• High-visibility clothing helps others see them.

• Hearing protection helps protect their ears from loud noises.

This equipment is called personal protective equipment, or PPE for short.

Safety Comes First

Before starting a job, utility workers look for possible dangers and decide how to protect themselves and others.

Getting the job done safely is just as important as getting the job done.

Did You Know?

Hard hats come in many different colors. Some companies use different colors to identify workers with different responsibilities.

Your Next Discovery

Remember, construction sites are not playgrounds. Always stay outside safety barriers.
