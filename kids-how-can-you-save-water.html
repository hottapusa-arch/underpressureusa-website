How Can You Save Water?

Piper noticed that Max had left the faucet running while brushing his teeth.

“Do you really need all that water?”

Max turned off the faucet and wondered what other little things he could do to save water.

Every Drop Matters

Water is a valuable natural resource.

It takes equipment, energy and hardworking people to collect, clean and deliver drinking water to our homes.

Using only the water we need helps protect our water supplies.

Small Changes Make a Big Difference

You can help save water every day by doing a few simple things:

• Turn off the faucet while brushing your teeth.

• Take shorter showers.

• Tell an adult when you notice a dripping faucet.

• Use a watering can instead of leaving a garden hose running.

• Turn off the water when you're finished using it.

Even small changes can make a big difference over time!

Watch for Hidden Leaks

Sometimes water is wasted without anyone noticing.

A leaking toilet or dripping faucet can waste water throughout the day and night.

Drip reminds everyone that finding and fixing leaks is an important part of saving water.

Saving Water Around Riverton

Water utility workers help save water, too!

They search for underground leaks, repair broken pipes and maintain the equipment that keeps water flowing throughout town.

Everyone has a part to play.

Did You Know?

A faucet that drips just once every second can waste more than 3,000 gallons of water in a year!
