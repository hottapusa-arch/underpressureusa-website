Why Are Manhole Covers Round?

Piper noticed a large, round metal cover in the street outside her house.

“Why is it round instead of square?”

Bolt was excited to explain the clever thinking behind this everyday object.

A Door to the Underground

A manhole provides an opening that allows trained utility workers to access underground systems.

Many manholes are connected to sewer pipes, allowing workers to inspect, clean and maintain them.

The heavy metal cover helps keep people safe and prevents unwanted objects from falling inside.

A Clever Shape

One reason many manhole covers are round is that a round cover cannot fall through its matching circular opening.

A square cover, however, could potentially fall through its opening if turned diagonally.

Round covers also don't need to face a particular direction when workers put them back into place.

Built to Be Strong

Manhole covers are often made from strong materials such as cast iron or ductile iron.

Covers installed in streets must be strong enough to support the vehicles that drive over them every day.

Not All Covers Are Round

Some underground utility openings have square or rectangular covers.

Their shapes and sizes depend on what equipment is underneath and how workers need to access it.

Did You Know?

Some manhole covers feature a town's name, a special design or even artwork!

Your Next Discovery

While walking with an adult, look for different utility covers along the sidewalk.

Can you find any words, numbers or interesting patterns?

Never try to lift a utility cover or climb inside an underground opening. That's a job for specially trained workers.

Keep exploring! There's an amazing world beneath your feet.
