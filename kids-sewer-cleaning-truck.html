Meet the Sewer Cleaning Truck

Max spotted a huge truck parked beside a manhole in Riverton.

It had a giant tank, a long hose and all sorts of interesting equipment.

“What does that truck do?”

Bolt couldn't wait to explain!

A Giant Cleaning Machine

Sewer cleaning trucks help utility workers keep underground sewer pipes clear.

Over time, grease, dirt and other materials can collect inside pipes, making it harder for wastewater to flow.

The Power of Water

Many sewer cleaning trucks use high-pressure water to clean the inside of sewer pipes.

A special nozzle attached to a long hose sprays powerful streams of water that help loosen and move unwanted material.

A Giant Vacuum

Some sewer cleaning trucks also have powerful vacuum systems.

Workers use these systems to collect water, dirt and debris from manholes and other underground structures.

The collected material is stored inside a large tank on the truck until it can be properly disposed of.

Preventing Problems

Utility workers don't always wait until a sewer pipe becomes blocked.

Regular cleaning helps prevent blockages, backups and sewer overflows.

These trucks are an important part of keeping Riverton's sewer system working.

Did You Know?

Some sewer cleaning trucks can carry more than 1,000 gallons of cleaning water!

Your Next Discovery

The next time you see a sewer cleaning truck, look for its large hose reel, storage tanks and vacuum hose.

Always watch from a safe distance while the workers do their job.
