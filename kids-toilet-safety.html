What Shouldn't Go Down the Toilet?

Max was helping clean up when he wondered if a cleaning wipe could go down the toilet.

“It's just like toilet paper, right?”

Piper contacted Drip to find out.

Toilets Aren't Trash Cans

Toilets are designed to carry human waste and toilet paper into the sewer system or a septic system.

Putting other things down the toilet can cause problems for the pipes beneath your home and throughout your community.

Remember the Three Ps

There are three simple things to remember:

• Pee

• Poop

• Toilet paper

It's that easy!

What About Wipes?

Toilet paper is designed to break apart in water.

Many wipes stay together much longer, even some labeled as flushable.

They can collect inside sewer pipes, clog pumps and cause problems at wastewater treatment plants.

The Trouble With Grease

Cooking grease can cool and build up inside sewer pipes.

When grease combines with wipes and other materials, it can create enormous blockages called fatbergs.

Bolt thinks that's a funny name for a very messy problem!

Did You Know?

Some sewer blockages grow so large that utility workers need special equipment to break them apart and remove them.

Your Next Discovery

Help your family remember the three Ps.

Keep a small trash can in the bathroom so everyone has somewhere to put wipes and other trash.

You can help protect the underground world right from your own home!
