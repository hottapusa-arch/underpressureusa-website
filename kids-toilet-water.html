Where Does Your Toilet Water Go?

Max flushed the toilet and watched the water disappear.

“Where does it all go?”

Drip explained that flushing the toilet is actually the beginning of another underground adventure!

Leaving Your Home

When you flush a toilet, take a shower or wash your hands, the used water flows down a drain.

This used water is called wastewater.

In communities like Riverton, underground sewer pipes carry wastewater away from homes, schools and businesses.

Gravity Helps Again!

Many sewer pipes are installed at a slight downhill angle.

Gravity helps wastewater flow through these pipes without needing a pump.

When wastewater needs to travel uphill, special pumping stations help move it along.

Time to Clean Up

Wastewater travels to a wastewater treatment plant, where workers and special equipment help clean it.

Even tiny living organisms help break down waste during the treatment process!

Bolt thinks it's amazing that something so small can do such a big job.

Back to Nature

After treatment, the cleaned water may be safely returned to a river or another body of water. Some communities even reuse treated wastewater for things like watering plants.

Not every home is connected to a public sewer system. Some homes use their own underground treatment systems called septic systems.

Did You Know?

The water pipes that bring drinking water into your home are separate from the sewer pipes that carry wastewater away.

Your Next Discovery

Remember the three Ps when using the toilet: pee, poop and toilet paper.

Things like baby wipes, toys and paper towels belong in the trash, not the toilet!
