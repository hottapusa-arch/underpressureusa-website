What's Beneath Our Streets?

Piper was walking through Riverton when she noticed a crew digging a large hole in the street.

“What are they looking for?”

Drip explained that there's an entire hidden world right beneath her feet!

A Hidden Network

Under our streets are pipes that carry drinking water to homes, schools and businesses.

Other pipes carry used water away from our sinks, showers and toilets.

There are also pipes that help carry rainwater away from streets and sidewalks.

More Than Just Water

Water pipes aren't the only things underground.

There may also be electrical cables, natural gas pipes and communication lines that help connect our homes to the internet.

All these underground systems help our towns and cities work.

Finding What's Underground

Before workers begin digging, underground utilities need to be located.

Workers use special equipment, colorful flags and painted markings to help identify where different utilities are buried.

Bolt thinks the equipment they use is pretty amazing!

Did You Know?

Blue markings identify drinking water lines, while green markings identify sewer and drain lines.

Your Next Discovery

The next time you're walking through your neighborhood with an adult, look for manhole covers, water valve covers and fire hydrants.

They're clues to the hidden world beneath your feet!

Keep exploring! There's an amazing world beneath your feet.
