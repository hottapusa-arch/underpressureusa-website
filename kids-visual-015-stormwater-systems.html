How Stormwater Systems Work

Collecting Rain

Rain that runs off roofs, streets and parking lots is called stormwater runoff. Storm drains, ditches and pipes help carry it away from places where it could collect and flood.

Where It Goes

Stormwater may travel to a stream, pond or other waterway. Some systems use ponds, planted areas or other features to slow runoff and help remove pollution.

Keeping Waterways Clean

Trash, oil and dirt on streets can wash into storm drains. Keeping litter off the ground helps protect rivers and the animals that live there. Never enter a storm drain or play in floodwater.

Did You Know?

Storm drains often lead to waterways rather than a wastewater treatment plant.
