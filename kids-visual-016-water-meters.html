How Water Meters Work

Select the illustration to view it at full size.

Max asks how the water department knows how much water a home uses. Bolt points to a water meter.

Counting Water

A water meter measures the amount of water that passes through it. It might count gallons or cubic feet, depending on the utility.

Where Meters Live

Meters may be in a box near the street, inside a building or in another protected location. Some can send readings electronically so workers do not need to visit each meter.

Finding Unusual Use

A meter reading helps a utility calculate water use. Unexpected use can also be a clue that a toilet, faucet or pipe is leaking. An adult or utility worker can investigate.

Did You Know?

A water meter measures volume, not water pressure.

Your Next Discovery

Ask an adult to show you a water bill. Can you find how much water was used?
