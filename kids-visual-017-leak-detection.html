How Water Leaks Are Detected

Select the illustration to view it at full size.

Mia wonders how workers find a leak when the pipe is hidden underground.

Looking for Clues

A wet spot on a dry day, unusual water use or a change in pressure can be a clue. But not every leak reaches the surface, and not every puddle comes from a water main.

Listening Underground

Water escaping from a pressurized pipe can create vibrations and sound. Workers use special listening equipment and sometimes compare sounds at different locations to narrow down the search.

Finding and Fixing the Problem

Crews combine clues with maps and other tools before deciding where to investigate. Finding leaks can save water and help prevent damage. Repairs are performed by trained workers.

Did You Know?

Some underground leaks can continue for a long time without a visible puddle.

Your Next Discovery

If you notice water bubbling out of a street, stay away and tell an adult so they can report it.
