Water in Motion

Water moving through a pipe has momentum, just like a rolling wagon. A sudden change in its speed can create a pressure wave that travels through the pipe.

A Sudden Stop

A quickly closing valve or a pump that suddenly stops can cause a pressure surge. Sometimes the pipes make a banging sound. Large surges can damage pipes or equipment.

Protecting the Pipes

Utility workers plan how valves and pumps operate. They may use special surge-control equipment to help manage pressure changes and protect the system.

Did You Know?

Water hammer can happen even when you cannot hear a bang.
