Three Tools for Working on Water Mains

Select the illustration to view it at full size.

Drip and Bolt compare three ways utility crews work on existing water mains.

Hot Tap: Add a Connection

A hot tap creates a new branch from a main while it remains in service. It can help bring water to a new building or street.

Line Stop: Temporarily Block Flow

A line stop places a temporary barrier inside a main. It can help isolate a section for repairs, sometimes with a bypass to keep other areas supplied.

Valve Insert: Add a Permanent Valve

A valve insert gives workers a permanent way to control water flow in the future. Each tool has a different purpose, and trained crews choose equipment based on the job.

Did You Know?

These three tools can help reduce service interruptions, but not every job can be done without an outage.
