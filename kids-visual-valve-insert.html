How Valve Inserts Work

Select the illustration to view it at full size.

Piper learns how a new permanent valve can be added to an existing water main.

A New Way to Control Water

A valve is like a gate inside a pipe. A valve insert adds a permanent valve where there was not one before. This can help crews control smaller sections of a water network.

A Special Fitting

Trained crews attach a sealed sleeve to the main and use specialized equipment to install a valve assembly. Many systems allow the work to take place while the main remains pressurized. The valve stays in the system after the work is finished.

Ready for the Future

If a pipe breaks later, the new valve may help workers isolate the damaged section. Other valves may also be needed to stop water arriving from different directions.

Did You Know?

A valve insert is permanent, unlike a temporary line stop.
