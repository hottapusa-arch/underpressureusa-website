Water Pressure Basics

Max wonders why water comes out of a faucet when he opens it. Drip explains pressure.

Water Has a Push

Pressure is the push water exerts against the inside of a pipe. When you open a faucet, a difference in pressure helps water move toward the opening.

Where the Push Comes From

Pumps add energy to water. Water stored high in a tower also creates pressure because of its weight. Both can help supply homes through underground pipes.

Pressure Is Not the Same as Flow

Pressure describes the push. Flow describes how much water moves in a certain amount of time. A closed faucet can have pressure behind it even when no water is flowing.

Did You Know?

Water pressure is often measured in pounds per square inch, or PSI.

Your Next Discovery
