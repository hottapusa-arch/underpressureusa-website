The Amazing Journey of a Water Drop

Mia watched a raindrop slide down her bedroom window.

“Where did that little drop come from?”

Drip smiled. That tiny drop of water might have been traveling around our planet for millions of years!

The Journey Up

When the sun warms water in oceans, lakes and rivers, some of it changes into an invisible gas called water vapor.

This process is called evaporation.

The water vapor rises into the air.

Making Clouds

As water vapor cools, it can change back into tiny drops of liquid water.

This process is called condensation.

When lots of these tiny droplets or ice crystals gather together, they form clouds.

Falling Back to Earth

As water droplets or ice crystals grow inside clouds, they can become heavy enough to fall.

Water returns to Earth as rain, snow, sleet or hail.

This is called precipitation.

Where Does It Go Next?

Some water flows into streams, rivers and oceans. Some soaks into the ground, and some is collected in reservoirs.

Water treatment plants can clean water and prepare it for its next adventure through the pipes beneath Riverton.

Eventually, water can return to nature and begin its journey again.

This never-ending adventure is called the water cycle!

Did You Know?

Some of the water you drink today may once have been part of a dinosaur's drinking water!

Your Next Discovery

With an adult, place a clear glass containing ice water on a table.

Watch as tiny water droplets form on the outside of the glass.

That water didn't leak through the glass. Water vapor in the surrounding air cooled and turned into liquid water.

You've just discovered condensation!

Keep exploring! There's an amazing world beneath your feet.
