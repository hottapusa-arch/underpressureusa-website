How Do Workers Fix Broken Water Pipes?

Max heard the rumble of a big excavator outside his house.

A water pipe had broken beneath the street, and a utility crew had arrived to fix it.

Max couldn't wait to find out how they would do it!

Finding the Problem

Before workers start digging, they need to find the broken pipe and locate other underground utilities.

Sometimes they use special listening equipment to hear water escaping from a pipe beneath the ground.

Controlling the Water

Workers can close underground water valves to stop or reduce the flow of water to the damaged pipe.

This is why your water might temporarily stop working during a repair.

Digging Down

An excavator helps workers remove soil and uncover the damaged pipe.

The crew uses special safety equipment to help protect everyone working around the excavation.

Making the Repair

Sometimes workers can place a special repair clamp around the damaged pipe.

Other times, they need to remove the broken section and replace it with a new piece of pipe.

Bolt loves seeing all the different tools and equipment workers use!

Getting Water Flowing Again

Once the repair is complete, workers follow important steps to make sure the water system is ready to return to service.

They fill the excavation, repair the street and get ready for their next job.

Did You Know?

Some specially trained workers can perform certain repairs and install new connections without shutting down the entire water system!

Your Next Discovery

The next time you see a water department truck, think about all the work that goes into keeping water flowing to your home.

These everyday heroes help keep our communities running!

Keep exploring! There's an amazing world beneath your feet.
