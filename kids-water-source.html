Where Does Your Water Come From?

Have you ever turned on the faucet and wondered where all that water comes from?

Piper had the very same question!

Getting water to your home is a much bigger adventure than you might imagine.

The Journey Begins

Your water might come from a lake, a river, a reservoir or even deep beneath the ground.

But before it reaches your faucet, it usually needs to be cleaned.

Getting Water Ready

At a water treatment plant, special equipment helps remove dirt and other things that shouldn't be in your drinking water.

Water professionals work hard to make sure your water is safe to drink.

The Underground Adventure

This is Drip's favorite part!

Water travels through a huge network of underground pipes. Pumps help move it, and water towers help store water and provide pressure.

Smaller pipes carry water from the large underground pipes into your home.

Every time you brush your teeth, take a shower or fill a glass of water, an entire water system is working behind the scenes.

Did You Know?

Some of the water pipes beneath your street may have been there before your grandparents were born!

Your Next Discovery

The next time you're riding through town, look for a water tower. Can you spot one?

That tower might be part of the very system that delivers water to your home!

Keep exploring! There's an amazing world beneath your feet.
