Why Do We Need Water?

After playing outside in Riverton, Max ran inside to get a glass of water.

“Why do we get so thirsty?”

Drip explained that water is one of the most important things our bodies need!

Water Inside You

More than half of your body is made up of water.

Water helps your body digest food, carry nutrients, control its temperature and remove waste.

When you run, play and sweat, your body loses water. Drinking water helps replace what you've lost.

Every Living Thing Needs Water

People aren't the only ones who need water.

Animals need water to survive, and plants use water to grow and make their own food.

From the smallest insect to the largest elephant, water is an important part of life.

More Than Just Drinking

Think about how many ways your family uses water every day.

You use water to brush your teeth, wash your hands, prepare food, clean clothes and take showers.

Farmers use water to grow food, firefighters use it to fight fires, and hospitals need it to care for patients.

Keeping Riverton Running

An entire community depends on water.

That's why so many people work hard to keep our water systems operating every day.

Piper never realized how important those hidden underground pipes were!

Did You Know?

A watermelon is more than 90 percent water!

Even some of the foods we eat help provide our bodies with water.
